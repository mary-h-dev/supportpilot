# Two-factor authentication
Enable it in Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Authy, then enter the 6-digit code.

When you enable it, Nestly shows 10 one-time backup codes. Save them: they are shown only once. If you lose your phone, sign in with one of the backup codes.

If you have neither your phone nor a backup code, contact support. After identity verification, two-factor authentication is reset within 2 business days.
