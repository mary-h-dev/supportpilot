# Refund policy
Nestly offers a full refund within 14 days of your first payment on any plan. Renewal payments are refundable within 3 days of the charge.

To request a refund, open Billing > Payment history, choose the charge and click "Request refund". Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

Refunds are not available for add-on purchases (extra storage, extra seats) after they have been used.
